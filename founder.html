About the Founder

Building a simpler way to learn modern technology.

B. Sriram Marudhaiyappan is the Founder of ENJOY CREATOR ACADEMY (ECA), a Tamil-focused digital learning platform dedicated to helping learners, creators and aspiring digital professionals learn modern Artificial Intelligence, content creation and digital skills.

Through ENJOY CREATOR ACADEMY, Sriram focuses on making modern technology simple, practical and accessible to Tamil-speaking learners.

THE FOUNDERAbout B. Sriram Marudhaiyappan

B. Sriram Marudhaiyappan is an entrepreneur and digital educator focused on the growing world of Artificial Intelligence, content creation and digital education.

With ENJOY CREATOR ACADEMY, his goal is to create practical learning experiences that help beginners understand modern AI tools and use them confidently in real-world projects.

His approach focuses on simple explanations, practical demonstrations and easy-to-follow learning resources.

One of the key areas of ENJOY CREATOR ACADEMY is AI Video Creation.
